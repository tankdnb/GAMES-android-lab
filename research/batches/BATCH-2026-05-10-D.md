## Lessons From This Batch

- Lightweight single-repo batches work well for compact repositories that are too important to skip but too small to justify a crowded mixed batch.
- Small samples can still be strong catalog entries when they separate gameplay rules, input, animation, HUD, and tests clearly enough to reuse.
- Even a minimal Gradle discovery attempt may fail for environment reasons unrelated to the repository itself, so the build-mode note should capture the exact failure shape instead of flattening everything into timeouts.
