- Initial selection reasons:
  - explicit MIT licensing
  - freshest remaining shortlist leader with non-zero public signal at `5` stars
  - recent code push on `2026-05-31`
  - Kotlin-dominant language mix with an `editor/`, `example/`, and `lib/` split that suggests enough architecture surface to justify a static-first pass
- Final verdict:
  - keep `aleksrutins/platinum` in the lab as `reference-only`
  - strongest reusable value is the tiny system-typed component model, the timer-driven outer loop, the rollback collision approach, and the callback-driven tilemap entity loader
  - main caveats are the missing README/docs/CI surface, the incomplete `Scene` integration, the placeholder editor module, the trivial test surface, and the lack of any direct Android target
