## Working Notes

- Main question for this batch: whether `piano-tiles` is only a narrow arcade clone or still contains reusable Android/Kotlin patterns around speed control, runtime flow, gesture/input handling, and simple game-state orchestration.
- Expected hotspots before cloning: `app`, activity or fragment entry points, custom `View` or Compose game surface, input handling, note/tile generation flow, score/speed progression, audio, and any persistence or configuration layer.
- Final verdict: `reference-only`
- The decisive factors were the direct Android `SurfaceView` runtime, device-height speed normalization, tactile sound/vibration loop, and the compact high-score-by-difficulty shell.
- The main caveats are the repository's narrow scope, complete lack of tests, GPL-3.0 licensing, heavy reliance on mutable companion state, and the fact that `GameThread` never exits cleanly after `running` is disabled.
