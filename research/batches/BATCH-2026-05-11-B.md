## Lessons From This Batch

- Final verdict: `accepted` as a real Kotlin multiplatform engine reference with direct Android runtime support, strong framebuffer/render-stage architecture, scene-to-ECS import, coroutine scripting, and meaningful common tests.
- Even a stale framework can outrank fresher GitHub noise when the newer results are mostly zero-signal samples and the older repository still exposes reusable engine seams with verified Android adapters.
- `miniGDX` is particularly valuable as a companion note to `minigdx/tiny`, because it documents the lower-level runtime, rendering, and platform abstractions behind that ecosystem.
- The batch also exposed two concrete caution points worth preserving in the note: likely Android multitouch indexing issues in `AndroidInputHandler` and suspicious scripted movement math in `ScriptContext.moveOf`.
