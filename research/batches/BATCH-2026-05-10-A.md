## Lessons From This Batch

- The full research workflow works end-to-end when the batch is limited to one Android game, one engine, one SDK, and one wildcard repository.
- Repository footprint should be checked before cloning; very large candidates like `yairm210/Unciv` should be deferred into dedicated heavy-repo batches.
- Gradle discovery is useful, but timeout is a valid documented outcome for third-party multi-module projects and should not block static-first research.
- The normalized category split between accepted catalog entries and `reference-only` entries was useful on the first pass and should be kept.
