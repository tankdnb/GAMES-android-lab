## Working Notes

- Main question for this batch: whether `compose-tetris` is only a visually popular Compose demo or a genuinely strong Android reference for frame stepping, input, collision/board logic, and UI layering.
- Expected hotspots before cloning: `app/src/main`, game loop/state holders, gesture/input handling, board/tetromino rules, scoring/progression, rendering/composition, and any tests or result artifacts.
- Final verdict: `accepted`
- The decisive factors were the reducer-like `ViewModel` loop, Compose `Canvas` board rendering, reusable held-button auto-repeat controls, pure tetromino transform logic with shuffled bag generation, and the compact line-clear animation/state pipeline.
- The main caveats are the stale toolchain (`Compose 1.1.1`, AGP `7.1.2`, `jcenter()`), almost-empty test surface, and a likely stale-level tick-speed bug caused by the constant-key `LaunchedEffect(Unit)` loop in `MainActivity.kt`.
