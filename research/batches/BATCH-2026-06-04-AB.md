## Working Notes

- Main question for this batch is whether `composelife` is just a polished simulator app or whether it also contains reusable Android game-development value for rendering, persistent state, grid simulation, and watchface or wearable product patterns.
- Initial selection reasons:
  - explicit Apache-2.0 licensing
  - current public signal at `253` stars
  - fresh code activity on `2026-06-03`
  - direct Android and watchface surface with expected Compose-state and rendering depth
- Final verdict:
  - keep `alexvanyo/composelife` in the main catalog as `accepted`
  - strongest reusable value is the explicit evolving simulation state, HashLife or naive algorithm hot-swap, AGSL or SKSL or OpenGL rendering family, Metro plus context-parameter DI shell, custom stateful navigation, and WorkManager-backed pattern sync flow
  - main caveats are the large monorepo footprint, the Windows checkout breakage caused by `:`-named watchface solution resources, and the need for JDK `17+` in general and effectively `JDK 21+` for normal upstream work
